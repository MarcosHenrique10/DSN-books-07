Bing Bong decided to have his tea party right there

in Storybook Land. He poured himself a cup of tea and

toasted the three Bing Bongs, who leaned out of their

books and toasted him right back.

“Cheers!” said Jungle Bing Bong.

“Here’s to you!” said Circus Bing Bong.

“Good health!” said Chef Bing Bong.

And so Bing Bong enjoyed a wonderful afternoon with

three good friends: Bing Bong, Bing Bong, and Bing Bong!
