“Fine!” said Mickey. “We’ll build you the house.
 How about one of these?”

“No, lad, no,” said Patrick Begorra. “I like a
 place out of sight, hidden away, so to speak.”

“All right,” said Mickey. “Just as you say.”

So they shook hands on it. The bargain was
 made. They all flew back to the Disneyland site.
 And next morning, the work began.
