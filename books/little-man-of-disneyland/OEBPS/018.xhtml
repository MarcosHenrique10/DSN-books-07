“Oh, no you don’t!” he cried in alarm. “I’ll not
 go in there. What is it, anyway?”

“It’s a helicopter, a kind of airplane. Come along
 for the ride,” said Mickey.

When he saw all the rest of them piling in,
 Patrick Begorra went, too. Soon, with a whirr and
 a rush of blades, up went the helicopter, straight up
 in the air, and the ground dropped away below.
