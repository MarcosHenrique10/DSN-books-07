“Rocket trip—flying pirate ship! You must be
 out of your head, my boy,” said Patrick Begorra.
 “There are no such things.”

“Come along and see. We’ll show you the plans.
 They’re back at the Studio,” said Mickey Mouse.

Before he knew what he was about, Patrick
 Begorra found himself walking along with
 Mickey and the rest. But he stopped short when
 they opened a door into a strange-looking bubble
 of glass.
