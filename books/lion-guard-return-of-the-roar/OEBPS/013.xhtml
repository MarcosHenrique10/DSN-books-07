Kion’s roar echoes throughout the Pride Lands,
 all the way to Pride Rock.

“Do you think that was Kion?” Simba asks Rafiki.
 “He can’t possibly be ready. He’s still a cub.”

At that moment, Kion arrives with Bunga.

“We have heard your roar!” Rafiki says excitedly.
 Rafiki explains that Kion has the Roar of the Elders.
 “The great lions of the past roared with you. Now it is
 time for YOU to lead the Lion Guard.”
