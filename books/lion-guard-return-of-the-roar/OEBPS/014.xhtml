Simba and Rafiki lead Kion and Bunga to a secret
 meeting place.

“The Lion Guard is the team that protects the Pride
 Lands and defends the Circle of Life,” Simba explains.
 “And this is where they used to meet.”

“Your great-uncle Scar also had the gift of the Roar,”
 Simba continues. “He wanted to get rid of his brother,
 Mufasa, so he could be king. But the Lion Guard
 refused to help Scar, so he used the Roar to destroy
 them. By using the Roar for evil, Scar lost its power
 forever.”
