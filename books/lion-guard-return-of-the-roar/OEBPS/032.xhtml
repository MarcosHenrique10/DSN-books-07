“Well, if it isn’t Kion the lion cub!” Janja sneers.

“We’re the Lion Guard, Janja,” Kion replies.
 “We defend the Circle of Life. You and your kind

Kion takes a deep breath and lets out
 a roar so powerful that it sends the
 hyenas running and stumbling back
