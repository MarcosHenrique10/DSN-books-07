Later that day, Simba visited his uncle Scar. “My dad just
 showed me the whole kingdom. And I’m going to rule it all!”
 he said excitedly.

With an evil grin, Scar asked if Mufasa had shown Simba
 the Elephant Graveyard beyond the northern border. “Only the
 bravest lions go there,” he added. Scar knew Simba couldn’t resist
 the chance to prove his bravery.
