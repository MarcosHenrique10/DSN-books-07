Reluctantly, Scar admitted to the animals of the Pride Lands
 that it was he who had killed Mufasa.

Victorious, Simba ordered Scar to leave the kingdom. But when
 Simba turned his back, Scar attacked him again!

With a swipe of his powerful paw, Simba knocked his uncle over
 the edge, where a pack of hungry hyenas was waiting. Scar was
 never seen in the savannah again.
