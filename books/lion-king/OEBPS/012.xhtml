Dark mist covered the ground. Simba and Nala cautiously
 walked toward an enormous skull. “We could get in big trouble,”
 Nala whispered.

Zazu, who had been searching for the cubs, knew they were in
 terrible danger. As soon as he found them, three hyenas emerged
 from the shadows and backed them into a corner!

“We’d love you to stick around for dinner,” said Shenzi, the
 leader of the trio.
