Scar, Mufasa’s brother, didn’t attend the ceremony. Scar had
 been next in line to rule until Simba was born. He wanted to get
 rid of the young cub so he could be king.

“Life’s not fair, is it?” Scar complained to a mouse he was
 about to eat.

Just then, Mufasa’s adviser, Zazu, flew by. “King Mufasa’s on his
 way,” he announced. Mufasa was not happy that Scar was absent
 from the celebration.
