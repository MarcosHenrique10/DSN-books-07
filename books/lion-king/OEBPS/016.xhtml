Meanwhile, Scar was angry that the hyenas had failed to kill
 Simba. “I practically gift wrapped those cubs for you,” he told
 them. But Scar had a new plan.

“Be prepared,” he announced. “For the death of the king.”

“Why? Is he sick?” asked a hyena named Ed.

“No, fool. We’re going to kill him. Simba, too,” he said.
