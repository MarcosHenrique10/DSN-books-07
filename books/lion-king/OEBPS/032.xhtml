Scar wouldn’t give up the throne that easily, so he backed
 Simba over the edge of Pride Rock. With his nephew dangling
 below, Scar whispered into Simba’s ear: “I killed Mufasa!”

Anger rushed through Simba’s body. With a mighty roar, he
 climbed over the edge and pinned Scar to the ground. “Tell them
 the truth!” he cried out.
