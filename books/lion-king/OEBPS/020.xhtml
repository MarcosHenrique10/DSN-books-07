Scar watched as Simba fled the only home he’d ever known.
 Once the cub was out of sight, Scar ordered the hyenas to kill
 him. They chased Simba through the gorge and up a steep cliff.

Desperate to escape, Simba leaped into a thorny thicket below.

The hyenas skidded to a stop when they saw the thicket.
 “He’s as good as dead out there,” they agreed before returning
 to Pride Rock.
