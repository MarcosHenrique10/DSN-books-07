The next day, Scar brought Simba to a gorge, where wildebeests
 were grazing nearby. “Now you wait here. Your father has a
 marvelous surprise for you,” he said.

When Scar was safely away, the hyenas chased the wildebeests
 toward Simba!

Then Scar ran off to find Mufasa. “Stampede! In the gorge!
 Simba’s down there!” he said. Mufasa immediately hurried off to
 save his son.
