“Sarabi and I didn’t see you at the presentation of Simba,”
 said the king. As Mufasa’s brother, Scar should have been first to
 greet the cub.

Scar shrugged and told the king that he forgot. Then he
 walked back to his den.

“Don’t turn your back on me, Scar,” said Mufasa.

“Perhaps you shouldn’t turn your back on me,” Scar warned.
