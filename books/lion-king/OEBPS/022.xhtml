Simba ran and ran until he couldn’t run anymore. Exhausted,
 he fainted under the heat of the desert sun, where a meerkat
 named Timon and a warthog named Pumbaa found him. The
 cheerful friends brought Simba home with them.

When Simba woke up, he was still sad, but Timon and Pumbaa
 explained that when life gets them down, a certain phrase helps
 them feel better. . . .
