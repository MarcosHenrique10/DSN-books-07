While Simba searched for Scar, Timon and Pumbaa
 distracted the hyenas. Simba soon found the false king arguing
 with his mother. And when Simba saw Scar strike her, he let
 out an angry roar and lunged at his uncle!

“Simba! I’m a little surprised to see you . . . alive,” said Scar,
 eyeing the hyenas.

“The choice is yours, Scar,” said Simba. “Either step down
 or fight.”
