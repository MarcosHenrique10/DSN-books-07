With a wave of his hand, the Genie summoned a stunning
lineup of diamonds, rubies, and emeralds. “Step right up
and choose your very own treasure of all treasures!” he
exclaimed.

Jasmine looked at each beautiful jewel, but somehow she
knew none of them was the treasure she was seeking.

“Thank you for trying, Genie,” she said. “I think I will
know it when I see it.”
