Suddenly, the coach stopped at a large, colorful tent that was set up at the end of a road. The street, it seemed, had been blocked by a traveling show.

“Oh, how fun!” Jasmine exclaimed as she read the sign posted near the tent. “There’s a circus in town! I’ll have to tell Aladdin as soon as we get home. And speaking of home,” she said, “we really should be going. It’s almost time for lunch.”
