So that very afternoon, Jasmine, Aladdin, Rajah, and Abu all went to the circus. The ring was full of exciting, marvelous acts. There were clowns and acrobats and sword-swallowing giants. There were dogs and horses and monkeys that could juggle and jump through hoops!

“This is wonderful!” exclaimed Jasmine. “Rajah, don’t you agree?”

But instead of looking delighted, Rajah looked more solemn than ever before.

“I thought he’d be happy,” Jasmine told Aladdin.

“He will,” said Aladdin. “Just wait.”
