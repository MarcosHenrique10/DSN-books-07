Back at the palace, however, Rajah hardly touched his lunch.

“Goodness,” Jasmine said to Aladdin. “This isn’t like Rajah at all.”

“What do you think is wrong with him?” Aladdin asked Jasmine. Neither of them had ever seen Rajah so quiet or sullen—or not hungry!—before.

But Jasmine could only shrug. “I haven’t the

faintest idea,” she replied.
