Mowgli, you see, had been left all
alone in the jungle as a baby. He was found
by Bagheera the panther. Bagheera could not give
the small, helpless Man-cub care and nourishment,
so he took the boy to the den of a wolf family with
young cubs of their own. And that is how it happened that
Mowgli, as the Man-cub came to be called, was raised among
the wolves.
