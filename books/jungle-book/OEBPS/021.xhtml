Mowgli was sulking by a large rock when a bear
named Baloo came along. Soon the two were playing
together. There were coconuts to crack, bananas to peel,
and sweet, juicy pawpaws to pick from jungle trees.

“You’re gonna make a swell bear!” Baloo
told Mowgli.

Mowgli was having such fun in the jungle!
Now he really didn’t want to leave!
