Bagheera and Baloo raced to the ruined city where the
monkeys made their home. They found Mowgli a prisoner
of Louie, king of the apes.

“What I desire is Man’s red fire,” King Louie told
Mowgli. “Now give me the secret so I can be like you.”

But before Mowgli could answer, Baloo and Bagheera
whisked him away.
