Time passed, and Simba grew. Early one morning,
 Mufasa took him to the top of Pride Rock. “Simba, look,”
 he said. “Everything the light touches is our kingdom.”

“Wow!” the young lion said. Then he asked, “What
 about the shadowy place?”

“That’s beyond our borders. You must never go there,
 Simba!” warned Mufasa.
