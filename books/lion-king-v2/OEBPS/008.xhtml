Later Simba proudly told his uncle, Scar, “Someday
 I’m gonna rule the whole kingdom! Well . . .
everything except the shadowy place. My father said
 I can’t go there.”

“He’s absolutely right,” Scar slyly agreed. “An
 elephant graveyard is no place for a young prince.
 Only the bravest lions go there.”

Scar knew Simba would want to prove that he was
 brave. He said nothing as Simba ran off to ask his
 friend Nala to explore the mysterious Shadow Lands
 with him.
