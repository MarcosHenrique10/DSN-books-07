When the stampede had passed, Simba found his
 father lying lifeless at the foot of a cliff. What Simba
 did not know was that Scar had pushed his brother off
 the rock!

“If it weren’t for you, the king would still be alive,”
 lied Scar, appearing at Simba’s side.

“He tried to save me,” said the cub, sobbing. “It was
 an accident.”

“Run away, Simba,” Scar advised. “Run away and
 never return.”
