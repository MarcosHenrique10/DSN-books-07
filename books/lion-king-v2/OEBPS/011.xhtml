When Zazu told the hyenas he was Mufasa’s minister,
 they realized that Simba was the future king.

“He’s a king fit for a meal,” Banzai snickered. The hyenas
 chased Simba and Nala.

Suddenly a thunderous ROARRRR! rattled rocks and
 bones. It was the roar of Mufasa, the Lion King! The
 frightened hyenas ran away.
