Timon and Pumbaa felt sorry for the cub. They took
 him to their jungle home and taught him how to live by
 Timon’s philosophy, hakuna matata, which meant
 “no worries.” They also taught the cub to eat insects.

Simba tried to put the past behind him. But one
 clear night, the stars reminded him of the old kings and
 his father, Mufasa.
