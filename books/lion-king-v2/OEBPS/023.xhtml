With an angry snarl, Scar forced Simba to the edge
 of a cliff. As Simba struggled to hold on, Scar leaned
 down and whispered, “Here’s my little secret, Simba.
 You didn’t kill your father. I did.”

Simba’s heart filled with rage, and the strength of
 ten lions surged through him. He struggled up onto
 the rock, leaped on Scar, and the battle began!
