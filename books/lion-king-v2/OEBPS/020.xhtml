Then one day a lioness chased Pumbaa. Simba
 rushed to protect his friend.

“Nala!” he cried.

“Simba?” the lioness said. “I thought you were
 dead.” The friends embraced. Then Nala told Simba
 that under cruel King Scar, the Pride Lands had
 become barren and the animals were starving. Hyenas
 were everywhere.

But Simba refused to go back and take his place as
 the Lion King. He did not feel worthy.
