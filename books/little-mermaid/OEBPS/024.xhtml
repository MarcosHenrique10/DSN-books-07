The following afternoon, Eric took Ariel for a rowboat ride across
 a lagoon. Sebastian knew that Ariel only had one more day to get the
 prince to kiss her or she would become Ursula’s prisoner forever. So he
 began conducting a sea-creature chorus to set the mood. “C’mon and
 kiss the girl. . . .” said the crab. “The music’s working!”

As the prince leaned toward Ariel, the boat suddenly tipped, and
 both Ariel and Eric fell into the water!
