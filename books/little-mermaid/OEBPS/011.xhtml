Meanwhile, deep in the ocean, the wicked sea witch, Ursula,
 used her magic to spy on Ariel. Ursula was bitter because King
 Triton had banished her from his kingdom for her evildoing.

“My, my . . . the daughter of the
 great sea king, Triton, in love
 with a human!” said Ursula.
“This headstrong, lovesick girl
 may be the key to my revenge
 on Triton.”
