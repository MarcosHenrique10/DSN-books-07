As the storm calmed down, Ariel dragged the unconscious
 prince to shore. “He’s still breathing. He must be alive,” she said.

Just then, a crab named Sebastian scuttled across the sand. He
 was the sea king’s music director and adviser. “Ariel, get away from
 that human!” he demanded. “Your father forbids contact with
 them, remember?”

“But Sebastian, why can’t I be part of his world?” Ariel asked.
 She then sang about longing to be with Prince Eric forever.
