At the surface, they found Scuttle flying over a large sailing ship.
 “Some celebration, huh, sweetie? It’s the birthday of the human
 they call Prince Eric,” Scuttle explained.

Forgetting her father’s rule, Ariel peered in amazement at the
 young man on the deck. “I’ve never seen a human this close. He’s
 very handsome,” said Ariel.

She watched as Eric’s adviser, Grimsby, presented the prince with
 a birthday gift.
