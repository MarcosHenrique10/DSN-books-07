Ariel swam to the water’s surface and found her seagull
 friend. “Scuttle, do you know what this is?” she asked,
 handing him the fork.

“Judging from my expert knowledge of humans,”
 he began, “it’s obviously a . . . a . . . dinglehopper!
Humans use these to straighten their hair.”

“Thanks, Scuttle!” said Ariel. “It’s perfect for my
 collection.” And with that, Ariel dove excitedly
 back underwater.
