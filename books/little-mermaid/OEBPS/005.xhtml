Once upon a time, a little mermaid named Ariel frolicked
 in the ocean. She loved to explore sunken ships and look for
 lost objects. She called to her friend Flounder, a yellow fish.
 “Come on, Flounder!” Ariel said. “I’m sure this old boat has
 lots of human treasure aboard.”

“I’m not g-g-going in there! It’s spooky!” said Flounder.

“Don’t be such a guppy! Follow me!” she told him.

When Ariel swam inside the ship’s cabin, she discovered
 a fork. “Oh, my gosh!” she gasped. “Have you ever seen
anything so wonderful?”
