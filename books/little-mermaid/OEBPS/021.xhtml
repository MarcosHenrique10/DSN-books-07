Ariel took a deep breath and nodded. The sea witch smiled
 deviously. “Oh, yes, I almost forgot. We haven’t discussed payment,”
 said Ursula. “I’m not asking much. All I want is—your voice!”

Ariel agreed, and Ursula completed the spell. The evil witch
 captured Ariel’s voice in a seashell locket and turned the mermaid
 into a human.
