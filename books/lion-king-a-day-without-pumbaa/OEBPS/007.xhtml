He was just about to pounce on a beetle when—

“Look out below!” Without any warning, Pumbaa swooped out of the
 trees on a vine and landed right on Timon. “Oops! Sorry, Timon,” he said,
 picking himself up.

“Sorry?” shouted Timon. “That’s the nineteenth—no twentieth time
 you’ve crashed into me this week! You’re a natural disaster! Why, you
 couldn’t catch a bug if it flew into your mouth.”

“Yes I could!” cried Pumbaa. “Just let me show ya.”
