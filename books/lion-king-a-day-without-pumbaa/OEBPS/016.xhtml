“Timon! Simba! Look what I found!”

Wham! Pumbaa tumbled out of the jungle,
 knocking into his friends. They crashed into a
 tree, and all the bugs Pumbaa had brought for
 Timon and Simba went flying into the air.

Embarrassed, Pumbaa climbed off his
 friends and faced them. “I came back to say
 I missed you. But now look what I’ve done.
 Guess, I’m the worst friend ever,” he said, sadly.
 Then he turned back toward the jungle.

“Wait!” cried Timon. “That’s not true!”

“You’re a great friend, and we really missed
 you!” said Simba. “We even missed your
 disasters.”

Timon grinned. “Yeah, it seems we’ve
 gotten used to being stepped on, bruised, and
 squashed on a daily basis.”
