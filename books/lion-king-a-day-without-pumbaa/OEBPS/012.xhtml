“We shouldn’t have been so hard on him,” said Simba.
 “I hope he’s okay.”

“He’s fine,” snapped Timon, who was still sore from
 getting squashed. “Besides, remember? He’s the one who
 walked out on us.”

Simba sighed.

“Oh, stop moping, kid,” Timon told him. “We can do
 anything we want now without any worries about getting
 run over or covered with mud. Let’s enjoy it while we can!”
