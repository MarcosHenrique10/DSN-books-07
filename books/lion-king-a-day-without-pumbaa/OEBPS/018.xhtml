Pumbaa looked at his friends hopefully. “You mean I can stay?”

“Sure! You’re our favorite disaster!” Simba exclaimed.

Pumbaa gleefully rushed toward them, accidentally knocking
 Timon over and stepping on Simba’s paw. But this time his friends
 didn’t mind at all.

As Simba curled up to sleep that night, he was already looking
 forward to the next day’s adventures—with both his friends.
