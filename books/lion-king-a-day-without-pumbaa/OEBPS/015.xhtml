“I don’t know,” said Simba.
 “What do you want to do?”

“I asked you first,” said Timon
 as the sun began to set. He had to
 admit that it was possible that he
 missed Pumbaa—a tiny bit.

By the time Simba and Timon
 finished dinner, they were both
 grumpy. They hadn’t really enjoyed
 anything they’d done that day. Now
 it was almost bedtime, and their
 friend still hadn’t returned.

“What do you want to do?”
 Timon asked gloomily.

“I don’t know. What do you want
 to do?” Simba replied.

They went back and forth . . .
again and again . . . until they heard
 a rustling sound.
