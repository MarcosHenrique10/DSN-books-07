The clumsy warthog lunged for a juicy grub, only to fall
 in a puddle, splattering Simba and Timon with mud.

“That’s it!” cried Timon. “I’ve had it! No more disasters!”

Pumbaa looked heartbroken. “Simba, do you think I’m a
 disaster, too?” he asked the lion cub.

“Well . . . sometimes the things you do are kinda
 disastrous,” Simba said.

“You’re right,” Pumbaa sighed. “It would be better for
 everyone if I just left.”
