“We need to keep moving,” Jasmine said. “Abu, you stay here and
 help Carpet. Aladdin, we have to get to the parade!”

Jasmine couldn’t see the parade anymore. But she could still hear
 it. “I think it’s over there,” she said, pointing.

She grabbed a long pole and vaulted from one rooftop to the next.
 Aladdin quickly followed!

Finally, they reached the building just before the end of the parade.

Three stories below, Jasmine saw the peacocks, the elephants—and
 yes—the tigers!
