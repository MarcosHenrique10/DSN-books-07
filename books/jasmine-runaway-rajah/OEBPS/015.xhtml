Just as Rajah was being loaded into a cage, Jasmine ran up and
 threw her arms around him.

“Rajah!” she cried. “I was so worried about you!”

Jasmine quickly removed his collar. “Come on, let’s go home.”

A deep voice stopped her. “Where do you think you’re taking my
 tiger?” The parade master stood in front of Jasmine.

Jasmine straightened up. “There’s been a mistake,” she said. “I am
 Princess Jasmine, and this is my tiger.”

“But he isn’t wearing the royal collar,” he pointed out.
