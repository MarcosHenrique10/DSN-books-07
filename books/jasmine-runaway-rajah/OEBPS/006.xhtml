Jasmine bought some fruit. She tossed a melon to Rajah. He
 flipped it up to his nose.

Then Jasmine tossed him an apple and a fig. Rajah balanced both
 on top of the melon!

At Jasmine’s signal, Rajah flung the fruit into the air. He sliced it
 into pieces with his claws. Jasmine caught the pieces in a bowl as they
 fell.

“Rajah, you’re good enough to be in the parade,” Aladdin said.
