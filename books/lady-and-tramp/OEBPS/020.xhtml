“It’s a rat!” cried Lady. “It ran up the side of the house
and into the baby’s room.”

In a flash, Tramp was inside the house, up the stairs,
and through the nursery door. The rat was about to jump
into the baby’s crib when Tramp leaped at it. There was a
terrible fight. Finally, Tramp trapped the rat and killed it.
Outside, Lady managed to pull free from her chain. She
raced inside the house and upstairs to the nursery.
