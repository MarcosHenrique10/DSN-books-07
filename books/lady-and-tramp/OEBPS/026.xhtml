A few months later, Jock and Trusty came to visit Lady
and Tramp. Jim Dear knew that Tramp had saved the
baby and had taken him in. Now Lady and Tramp were
the proud parents of four little puppies.

“They’ve got their mother’s eyes,” said Trusty. He
looked fondly at three of the tiny puppies.

“Aye, but there’s a bit of their father in them, too,” said
Jock, trying to stop the fourth puppy from chewing his
coat!

Lady and Tramp smiled. They were very proud of their
new family.

Tramp looked down and thought of how his life had
changed in a year. He couldn’t be happier. And he knew
they would all live happily ever after.
