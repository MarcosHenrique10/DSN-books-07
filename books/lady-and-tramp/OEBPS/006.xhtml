A few weeks later, Jock and Trusty found Lady lying
by her water bowl. She looked very sad.

“What’s wrong, lassie?” asked Jock.

Lady explained that Jim Dear and Darling seemed
upset with her—but she didn’t know why.

“I wouldn’t worry,” Jock said. “Darling is expecting a
wee bairn.”

Lady looked confused.

“He means a baby,” Trusty explained.

“Oh!” said Lady, still confused. “What’s a baby?”

“A baby,” Jock began, “is a cute little bundle of—”

But before he could finish, a voice behind him said,
“. . . trouble.”

Lady turned around to see who had spoken. Walking
toward her was a scruffy but handsome dog called
Tramp. “Remember, when a baby moves in, the dog
moves out!” he warned.
