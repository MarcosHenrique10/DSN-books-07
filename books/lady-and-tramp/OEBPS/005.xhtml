When Lady was six months old, she was given a beautiful
blue collar. She was so proud, she rushed out to show it to
her friends Jock and Trusty. Jock was a small black Scottish
terrier. Trusty was a big brown bloodhound. He had once
been a famous police dog, but now he was old and he’d lost
most of his sense of smell.

“My, you’re a full-grown lady now!” said Jock.

Lady felt that she was the luckiest, happiest dog in the
whole world.
